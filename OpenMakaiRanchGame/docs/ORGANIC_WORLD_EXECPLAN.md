## Corrected direction

- A naturally developed settlement is explained by terrain, access, parcels and use, not compulsory crooked houses and curves. Straight roads and aligned courtyards are allowed.
- Existing compact clusters are reusable starting cores, not final world bounds. The larger coherent coastal region and growth reserves are now explicit user goals.
- Separate gameplay service IDs from exterior plots. Kitchen and ranch office belong in the main house; services must survive the move, not force separate small buildings.
- Own houses and important public uses require meaningful interiors. A facade whose only purpose is to open the entire management UI is not the long-term target.
- Local asset modeling/integration has priority over another generic world generator. The requested town impression does not mandate 300 fully represented people.
- Civic growth needs before/construction/after assets, space and useful outcomes. Resource binding remains subject to LW-09/LW-12, not fabricated by the scenery exporter.
